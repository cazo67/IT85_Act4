# Live Demo Checklist

Authority: [official activity, section 11](../../BCIS_Subscription_Billing_and_Collection_Laboratory_Activity.md). Record server/client names, version/commit, API URL, test roles, printer, environment and evidence folder without passwords. This is a rehearsal plan, not passed evidence.

## Preparation

- Resolve packaged connectivity, retry/session isolation and service-control defects.
- Prepare recommended five role-covering users, three billing months, ten overdue accounts across aging buckets, mixed approved payments, a reversal and two service-control scenarios.
- Existing subscriber/service/plan/collector/area counts are sufficient; current financial scenario coverage is incomplete.
- Follow [AT-01–AT-12](AT-01-AT-12.md) and retain expected/actual database and report assertions.

## Required sequence

1. Start API/PostgreSQL, show live/ready and sign in as administrator.
2. Create/open a subscriber with Internet/Cable services and show monthly invoice/ledger debit.
3. Post exact/partial Cash and print/preview the receipt; retain command keys across uncertain responses once corrected.
4. Verify/post GCash with proof and demonstrate duplicate-reference handling after policy approval and implementation. This remains blocked, not an optional omission.
5. Show ledger and date-range SOA; demonstrate advance credit according to its approved rules for AT-03.
6. Show overdue/aging filters and controlled suspension/reconnection with appropriate actors.
7. Complete a balanced collector batch and demonstrate a visible shortage that cannot silently close as balanced. Settlement approval is separate from AT-08.
8. Reverse an eligible payment with reason and inspect its preserved receipt/history/audit effects.
9. Export and open management PDF/XLSX reports; reconcile totals and demonstrate printing.
10. Demonstrate lower-role restrictions and direct forbidden admin API calls.
11. Create/verify a backup and demonstrate the tested isolated restore procedure and financial comparison.
12. Exercise simultaneous office clients, restart/reconnect and interrupted submission without duplicates.
13. Present architecture, security, financial invariants, limits and retained evidence.

Do not simulate blocked features or label incomplete cases passed. Final acceptance requires the activity's applicable workflows and evidence or an explicit instructor exclusion.
